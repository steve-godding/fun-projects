Before you board tonight

On home WiFi, while you still have it

These six need a download inside the app after installing. Doing them in the air or on hotel WiFi is slower and, for the offline packs, painful.

  • Google TranslateiPhone · iPad

    The one app you will actually use. Open it and download the Japanese and Korean offline packs now — camera translation of menus and signs then works with no signal at all.

  • Google MapsiPhone · iPad

    Best transit router in Japan, down to the platform and transfer. Steve already has the Tokyo and Yokohama offline area downloaded, so nobody needs to repeat it — offline maps are per-device, so only bother on your own phone if you want maps working when you are off on your own with no signal.

  • Princess CruisesiPhone only

    Medallion, boarding pass, excursions and the pre-cruise notices. Watch it for how Princess handles Busan immigration on 10 Sep. No iPad version exists.

  • Suica in Apple WalletiPhone · Watch

    Not an App Store download. Wallet → + → Transit Card → Suica, then top up ¥5,000 with Apple Pay. Tap to ride trains and buses and to pay at any convenience store. Set it up on all four phones.

  • TripItiPhone · iPad

    Holds flights, hotel and cruise in one offline itinerary. Already on your Mac, so sign in on the phone and it syncs.

  • Safety tipsiPhone only

    Japan's official English-language earthquake, tsunami and typhoon alert app, built for visitors. September is peak typhoon season and you are at sea for ten days of it.

Also do these before the airport

Load Visit Japan Web in Safari and check both QR codes render — they must be pulled live, a screenshot is no longer accepted at the kiosk. Download whatever you plan to watch or read on the 11-hour Perth–Singapore–Narita run, because Singapore Airlines WiFi will not carry it.

Hotel WiFi, 3–4 Sep

Once you are on the ground

None of these need pre-loading. Install them at the Shiba Park Hotel over breakfast.

  • ecbo cloakiPhone · iPad

    Books luggage storage in shops and stations by the hour. This is the fix for arrival day — you land at 08:00 and cannot check in until 15:00, and nobody wants to drag cases around Shibakoen for seven hours.

  • GOiPhone · iPad

    Japan's taxi-hailing app, in English, card on file so there is no fare conversation. Worth having for the days when walking the last kilometre is not on — it turns a long slog into a two-minute ride.

  • Japan Travel by NAVITIMEiPhone only

    Transit specialist that compares IC-card and rail-pass fares properly, which Google Maps does not. For the iPad, Jorudan Japan Transit Planner does the same job.

  • Naver PapagoiPhone · iPad

    Korea's own translator, noticeably better than Google on Korean. One app for one day — Busan, 10 Sep.

  • VoiceTraiPhone · iPad

    Built by Japan's national research institute for spoken Japanese. It shows you the back-translation, so you can see whether it understood you before the other person has to. Needs a connection.

  • Windy.comiPhone · iPad

    Typhoon tracks and swell forecasts. Better than Apple Weather for the question you will actually be asking at sea, which is whether the ship still calls at the next port.

  • TabelogiPhone only

    The local restaurant scoring standard. Anything above 3.5 is genuinely good — Japanese reviewers mark hard, so do not read it as a five-star scale.

  • Xe CurrencyiPhone · iPad

    Caches the rate so it converts offline. Optional — Siri and the Calculator app will do it too.

Flight and sea days

Mostly the iPad's job

Ten nights aboard, five of them with a sea day either side. Download the content, not just the app.

  • Amazon KindleiPhone · iPad · Mac

    Already on your Mac. Sync the library to the iPad before you fly; ship WiFi is metered and slow.

  • AudibleiPhone · iPad

    Download the titles, not just the app. Good for the Singapore leg and for shore days where you are waiting around.

  • NetflixiPhone · iPad

    Downloads expire and are region-locked, so queue them tonight in Perth. Streaming from the ship is not realistic.

  • LINEiPhone · iPad

    Japan's default messenger. Only worth it if a tour operator or restaurant asks for it — otherwise skip.

Your Mac needs nothing

Checked against the Mac App Store: Google Translate, Google Maps, Papago, Princess and Organic Maps have no Mac build at all, and Kindle, Flighty, TripIt, 1Password, Obsidian and ExpressVPN are already installed. There is no Mac install to do before you leave.

Every link points at a verified Australian App Store listing. All apps listed are free to install.
