LOUNGE ROOM DESCENDS INTO CHAOS

Connolly Puppy Linked to Widespread Toy Incident

By Our Chief Crime CorrespondentFull report · Pages 2, 3 and 4

CONNOLLY – A quiet afternoon came to an abrupt end this week after a local lounge room was discovered covered in toys, balls and household items apparently displaced during what witnesses described as “an enthusiastic puppy operation.”

Investigators quickly turned their attention to Delilah Margaret, who was observed near the scene shortly before the disorder was discovered.

Evidence includes scattered toys, displaced cushions and a complete disregard for household organisation.

“She’s completely innocent.”Owner Kaitlyn

Owner Kaitlyn has firmly rejected all allegations.

“She’s completely innocent,” Kaitlyn stated while providing Delilah with cuddles and snacks.

At the time of publication, Delilah remained free, highly affectionate and entirely unconcerned by the investigation.
