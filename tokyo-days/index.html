Our Days in Tokyo

A first look at how we might spend our time in the city — gentle on the feet, easy to change. A starting point for a chat, not a locked-in plan.

For Kim, Shirley & Charles · from Steve

We get a little slice of Tokyo at each end of the cruise. It isn't a lot of time, so the idea is to keep it relaxed and see a few really good things rather than rush around.

Good news about the first day. We land at Narita early — around 8am — and the hotel has agreed to an early check-in, so the rooms should be ours as soon as we get there. That takes all the pressure off: straight in, a shower after the overnight flight, and a proper lie-down. Anything we do beyond that is a bonus.

Then we have one proper full day before we board the ship. Everything below is built around comfortable pacing — taxis doing the legwork, a proper sit-down lunch, and rest stops built in. Tip: hover over a photo (or tap it on your phone) to read more about each place.

How the Tokyo days shape up

3Thu Sep

Arrival & easing in

Land Narita ~8am · early check-in arranged
Gentle

A soft landing. We make our way from the airport to the hotel (about 70–90 minutes) and go straight into the rooms — no waiting about with the bags. Shower, unpack, sleep if we want to. Both spots below are a short flat walk from the hotel, with plenty of places to sit, for whenever we feel like stirring.

  • MorningNarita → hotel, ShibakoenTrain or airport bus. Straight into the rooms at the Shiba Park Hotel — shower, unpack, rest.
  • AfternoonZōjō-ji Temple & Tokyo TowerBoth a short, flat stroll from the hotel, once we've surfaced — see below.
  • EveningEarly, easy dinner near the hotelThen a good sleep to reset the body clock.
No pressure — do as much or as little as we feel
4Fri Sep

Shibuya — the big city day

Our one full day · the city, the views & a bit of shopping
Full day

Our proper day out, centred on the famous Shibuya Scramble Crossing. Taxis top-and-tail the day so there's no wrestling with the station, the shopping is all lift-served, and the walking stays flat with rests along the way.

  • 10:30Taxi to Shibuya~15–20 min door to door — drops us right at street level.
  • 10:45Scramble Crossing & Hachikō statueThe iconic crossing and the famous loyal-dog statue. Classic photos.
  • 11:15Shibuya Sky rooftop ★ highlightOpen-air rooftop over the whole city — Mt Fuji on a clear day. We'd book a timed ticket ahead. A free alternative exists — the Shinjuku government building, see the last day below.
  • 12:30Sit-down lunchThe restaurant floors of a department store — calm, comfortable, something for everyone.
  • 13:45Shopping at your own paceAll indoors and lift-served — browse as much or as little as you fancy.
  • 15:30Taxi back to the hotelTime to rest and repack before the cruise.
Pace built for comfort — nothing rushed, rests built in

15Tue Sep

One last day in Tokyo

Ship returns Yokohama early · night flight home from Haneda
Open day

The cruise arrives back into Yokohama early in the morning, and our flight home isn't until late that night — so we have a whole final day up our sleeves. Nothing planned yet: a lovely chance to tick off anything we missed, or just take it slow before the long flight. Ideas very welcome.

One free idea worth a look: the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building in Shinjuku offers observation decks with a Shibuya-Sky-like panorama — at no charge.
